APSRTC Live

Privacy and how it works

APSRTC Live is a free, crowdsourced bus tracker. Information comes from riders and is not official APSRTC information.

How it works

A rider can choose to share their bus's location while they are on the bus. Other people can then see the shared location on the map.

Information used while sharing

Only when someone shares, we collect the bus number, a session code, the bus type, latitude and longitude, and the time of the latest location update. Riders can also submit a capacity report: empty, half-full, or full.

Location sharing starts only after the rider taps to start, and continues only while sharing is on. Each session stores the latest position, replacing it with each update. The last position from a finished session may remain stored until old records are cleared.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or account details. There are no ads.

Storage and use

Shared data is stored with Supabase. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Your device stores your theme, language, recent searches, last selected bus type, and active sharing session in localStorage.

Accuracy and safety

Location accuracy is not guaranteed. Do not rely on APSRTC Live for urgent travel or safety decisions.
