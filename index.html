A quick note about location sharing

  • Location sharing is optional and starts only when you tap “I'm on this bus”.
  • Your browser may ask you to allow location access; you may need to enable it in your browser settings.
  • Please share only when you are genuinely riding that bus so the information stays trustworthy.
  • Remember to stop sharing, or let it expire automatically, when your ride is done.
  • Your location is not linked to your identity and is used only to show live bus positions to other riders.
  • Accuracy depends on your device's GPS and network. We do our best, but cannot guarantee perfect accuracy; sorry for any inconvenience.
  • For the best experience, please keep this tab open while sharing your location - switching apps may pause your updates. Sorry for the inconvenience, we're working to improve this soon! 🙏
  • 🙌 Thank you for helping fellow riders. Your contribution makes it easier for everyone to travel with confidence. 💙

              Privacy and how it works
              About

              APSRTC Live is a free community bus tracker for Visakhapatnam. It is not officially affiliated with APSRTC.

              How it works

              Search for your bus to see its live location when someone shares it. If you are riding, tap “I'm on this bus” to help others.

              Privacy

              Location is shared only when a user chooses to share. It expires automatically and is not linked to personal identity.

              Disclaimer

              This community tool depends on rider participation. Location may not always be available or accurate.

              Contact

              Report issues or send feedback to apsrtclive@gmail.com.
