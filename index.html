        About APSRTC Live

        APSRTC Live is a free, crowdsourced bus tracker for Visakhapatnam. It helps riders know where their bus is in real time using GPS data submitted by riders.

        This is a community project and is not officially affiliated with APSRTC.

        How it works

        Search for your bus number to see its route and live location when someone is sharing it. If you are currently riding, tap “I'm on this bus” to help other riders.

        Privacy

        Location data is collected only when you explicitly tap “I'm on this bus”. It is shared temporarily, expires automatically, is not linked to your personal identity, and is used only to show live bus positions to other users.

        Disclaimer

        This is a crowdsourced community tool. Location accuracy depends on user participation and may not always be available or accurate.
